Privacy Policy

At Pace WISP, we recognize that privacy is important. This Privacy Policy applies to all of the products, services, and websites offered by Pace WISP.

1. Information We Collect

In order to provide our full range of services, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: When you sign up for Pace WISP, we ask for personal information such as your name, email address, and company details.
  • Customer Data: For billing purposes, we store information about your end-users, including phone numbers, usage logs, and payment history.
  • Network Data: We collect technical data from your routers to provide monitoring and automated billing services.
  • Payment Information: Transaction details from M-Pesa, debit/credit cards, or bank transfers processed through our platform.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and improve our services.
  • To process transactions and send automated invoices.
  • To provide technical support and troubleshoot network issues.
  • To generate business analytics and growth reports for your WISP.
  • To ensure compliance with local telecommunications regulations.

3. Data Security

We take appropriate security measures to protect against unauthorized access to or unauthorized alteration, disclosure, or destruction of data. These include internal reviews of our data collection, storage, and processing practices and security measures, including appropriate encryption and physical security measures.

4. Information Sharing

Pace WISP only shares personal information with other companies or individuals outside of Pace WISP in the following limited circumstances:

  • We have your consent.
  • We provide such information to our integrated partners (such as M-Pesa or Banks) for the sole purpose of processing payments on your behalf.
  • To comply with any applicable law, regulation, or legal process.

5. Accessing and Updating Personal Information

When you use Pace WISP services, we make good faith efforts to provide you with access to your personal information and either to correct this data if it is inaccurate or to delete such data at your request if it is not otherwise required to be retained by law or for legitimate business purposes.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data as long as your account is active. Transactional logs are kept for a minimum period as required by financial regulations in Kenya and other regions of operation.

7. Changes to this Policy

Please note that this Privacy Policy may change from time to time. We will post any Policy changes on this page and, if the changes are significant, we will provide a more prominent notice.

8. Contact Information

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please feel free to contact us through our customer support channels or email us at support@pace.com.
